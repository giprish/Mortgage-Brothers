VA loans are powerful tools for veterans, active-duty service members, and eligible surviving spouses to achieve homeownership. These 

government-backed mortgages

 offer unique benefits that make buying a home more accessible and affordable.

Designed to honor military service, VA loans provide 

exceptional advantages

 over conventional financing options. With features like no down payment requirements and lower interest rates, these loans can significantly reduce financial barriers to homeownership.

From reduced closing costs to no private mortgage insurance, the benefits of VA loans can translate into 

thousands of dollars saved

 over the life of your mortgage. Let's explore the key features that make VA loans an unparalleled opportunity:

Get answers to frequently asked questions about VA loans. Our team has compiled the most common questions from veterans and service members about the VA loan process.

[/et_pb_text][et_pb_accordion _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" width="80%" module_alignment="center" custom_margin="||||false|false" custom_padding="||||false|false" global_colors_info="{}"][et_pb_accordion_item title="Can I use my VA loan benefits multiple times?" open="on" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243;]

Yes, even if you currently have a VA loan, you may be eligible for another VA loan with 0% down. Eligibility depends on your remaining entitlement and the county where you plan to purchase.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What types of properties can I buy with a VA loan?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

VA loans can be used for existing homes, new construction, approved condominiums, and owner-occupied multi-family properties up to 4 units.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Can I refinance my current mortgage with a VA loan?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Yes, you can refinance both VA and non-VA loans using either an Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan (IRRRL) or a VA cash-out refinance.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Can I use my VA loan for high-end properties?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Yes, VA jumbo loans are available for loan amounts up to $2,000,000 for borrowers with sufficient entitlement. Veterans with full entitlement have no VA loan limit -- a down payment is only required if your entitlement has been reduced by a previous VA loan.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Can I take cash out with a VA loan?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Yes, qualified veterans can access up to 100% of their home's value through a VA cash-out refinance for home improvements, debt consolidation, or other needs.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What credit score do I need for a VA loan?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

While the VA doesn't set a minimum credit score, most lenders require a score of at least 620. VA loans often offer competitive rates regardless of credit score.
