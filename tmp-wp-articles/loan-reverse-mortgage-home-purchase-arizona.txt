A Reverse Mortgage for Home Purchase is a unique financial tool that empowers seniors 62 and older to buy their dream home 

without the burden of monthly mortgage payments

. This innovative program combines a one-time investment of your own funds with the proceeds from a reverse mortgage to purchase a new primary residence.

By leveraging your home equity, you can potentially secure a 

more comfortable living situation

, move closer to family, or find a home that better suits your needs in retirement. This approach allows you to 

maintain ownership of your new property

 while preserving your cash flow for other important expenses.

Whether you're looking to downsize, upgrade, or relocate, a Reverse Mortgage for Home Purchase offers the flexibility to make these changes without straining your retirement savings. It opens up possibilities for 

higher-value homes or more desirable locations

 that might be out of reach with traditional financing.

This program not only helps you secure a new home but also provides 

financial freedom in retirement

. You can allocate funds towards healthcare, travel, or simply enjoying your golden years to the fullest, all while living in a home that meets your current needs and preferences.

With a Reverse Mortgage for Home Purchase, you're not just buying a house – you're investing in your quality of life and securing a comfortable future in a home that truly feels like yours.

Your long-term financial goals

Your health and potential future care needs

Your desire to leave an inheritance

I always advise my clients to involve their family members in the decision-making process. It's also important to consider how this financial move fits into your overall retirement strategy.

At The Mortgage Brothers, we've streamlined the reverse mortgage process to make it as easy as possible for you. Our expertise in the Arizona market ensures a smooth experience from start to finish. Here's how it works:

[/et_pb_text][et_pb_accordion _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" width="80%" module_alignment="center" custom_margin="||||false|false" custom_padding="||||false|false" global_colors_info="{}"][et_pb_accordion_item title="How does a Reverse Mortgage for Home Purchase differ from a traditional reverse mortgage?" open="on" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243;]

While both allow homeowners 62+ to tap into home equity, a Reverse Mortgage for Home Purchase is specifically used to buy a new home. Traditional reverse mortgages are for current homeowners who want to access their existing home's equity.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What happens to the home after I pass away?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Your heirs have options. They can keep the home by paying off the loan balance, sell the home to repay the loan, or turn the home over to the lender. Any remaining equity after the loan is repaid belongs to your heirs.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Can I sell the home if I choose to move?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Yes, you can sell your home at any time. The loan becomes due when you sell, and any remaining equity is yours to keep.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Do I have to make any payments while living in the home?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

No monthly mortgage payments are required. However, you must continue to pay property taxes, homeowners insurance, and maintain the home.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="How much down payment is typically required?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

The down payment is usually around 50-60% of the home's purchase price, but this can vary based on factors like age and current interest rates.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Will this affect my Social Security or Medicare benefits?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Generally, a Reverse Mortgage for Home Purchase does not affect Social Security or Medicare benefits. However, it may impact needs-based benefits like Medicaid.
