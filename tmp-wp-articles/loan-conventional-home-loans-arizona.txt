flexibility, competitive rates, and accessibility

With features like 

low down payments, high loan limits, and favorable terms

 for various property types, conventional home loans stand out as a versatile financing solution in today's real estate market. They're designed to serve the residential community, accommodating 

primary residences, vacation homes, and investment properties

. Conventional loans also offer unique benefits such as the ability to 

finance manufactured homes and condos

 with ease, making them an attractive option for a wide range of buyers.

One of the most compelling aspects of conventional home loans is that they represent 

some of the cheapest money you can borrow

. Thanks to the backing of Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac, these loans offer interest rates that often outcompete other financing options. This makes conventional loans an excellent choice for borrowers who value cost-effectiveness and long-term financial planning in their home purchase or refinance.

A conventional loan is a mortgage that is not backed by a government agency. These loans are typically owned by Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac and follow their guidelines. Conventional loans offer competitive rates and flexible terms for various property types and purposes.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What are the loan limits for conventional home loans in Arizona?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" hover_enabled="0&#8243; global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="on" sticky_enabled="0&#8243;]

For 2026, the conventional loan limits in Arizona are:

Single-family homes: $832,750

Duplexes: $1,066,250

Triplexes: $1,288,800

Four-unit properties: $1,601,750

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What's the minimum down payment for a conventional loan?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

You can put down as little as 3% for a conventional loan. This low down payment option makes homeownership more accessible, especially for first-time buyers. However, putting 20% or more down eliminates the need for private mortgage insurance (PMI).

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What credit score do I need for a conventional loan?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

The minimum credit score required for a conventional loan is 620. However, higher credit scores often lead to better interest rates and lower mortgage insurance premiums. It's beneficial to aim for the highest score possible to secure the best terms.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Can I use a conventional loan for an investment property?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Yes, conventional loans can be used for investment properties, second homes, and primary residences. This flexibility is one of the key advantages of conventional loans compared to some government-backed loan programs.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What is the recast option in conventional loans?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

The recast option is a unique feature of conventional loans that allows you to recalculate your mortgage payment after making a large principal payment. This can potentially lower your monthly payments without the need to refinance.
