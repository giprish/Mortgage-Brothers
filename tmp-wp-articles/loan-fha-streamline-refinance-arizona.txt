Understanding the FHA Streamline Refinance process can be complex. To help you navigate this option, we've compiled answers to some of the most common questions our clients ask:

[/et_pb_text][et_pb_accordion _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" width="80%" module_alignment="center" custom_margin="||||false|false" custom_padding="||||false|false" global_colors_info="{}"][et_pb_accordion_item title="Who qualifies for an FHA Streamline Refinance?" open="on" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243;]

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Will my mortgage insurance premium change?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Your mortgage insurance premium may change depending on when your current FHA loan was originated. Loans closed before May 31, 2009, have lower upfront and annual mortgage insurance rates compared to those closed after this date. For example, a 30-year fixed FHA Streamline refinance for loans before May 31, 2009, has a 0.01% upfront and 0.55% annual rate, while loans after this date have 1.75% upfront and 0.60% annual rates.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Can I get cash out with an FHA Streamline Refinance?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

No, the FHA Streamline Refinance program does not allow for cash-out refinancing. This program is designed specifically to lower your interest rate or adjust your loan term, not to access home equity.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Is an appraisal required for an FHA Streamline Refinance?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

One of the major benefits of the FHA Streamline Refinance is that no appraisal is required. This means you can refinance regardless of your home's current equity position, making it an attractive option for homeowners in various market conditions.
