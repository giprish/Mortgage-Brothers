Understanding FHA loans can be complex, but we're here to help clarify the most common questions. Here are some key points to know about FHA loans in Arizona:

[/et_pb_text][et_pb_accordion _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" width="80%" module_alignment="center" custom_margin="||||false|false" custom_padding="||||false|false" hover_enabled="0&#8243; global_colors_info="{}" sticky_enabled="0&#8243;][et_pb_accordion_item title="What's the minimum down payment for an FHA loan?" open="on" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243;]

The minimum down payment is 3.5% of the purchase price.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Are gift funds allowed for FHA loans?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Yes, borrowers can receive all of their down payment, closing costs, and prepaid taxes and insurance from a gift

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What credit score do I need for an FHA loan in Arizona?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Most lenders require a minimum credit score of 620, though some may go as low as 580 in certain cases.

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="How long do I have to pay mortgage insurance on an FHA loan?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

For loans with less than 10% down, mortgage insurance is required for the entire loan term

For loans with 10% or more down, mortgage insurance can be removed after 11 years of on-time payments

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What are the current FHA loan limits in Arizona?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" hover_enabled="0&#8243; global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off" sticky_enabled="0&#8243;]

Here are the 2026 FHA loan limits for Maricopa County, Arizona:

1‑Unit (One-Family): $557,750

2‑Unit (Two-Family): $714,000

3‑Unit (Three-Family): $863,100

4‑Unit (Four-Family): $1,072,600

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="Can I use an FHA loan to buy a condo?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Yes, but the condo project must be FHA-approved, which can be challenging in Arizona

[/et_pb_accordion_item][et_pb_accordion_item title="What's the maximum seller contribution on an FHA loan?" _builder_version="4.27.4&#8243; _module_preset="default" body_font_size="16px" global_colors_info="{}" toggle_level="h3&#8243; open="off"]

Sellers can contribute up to 6% of the sales price towards a buyer's closing costs and prepaids
